In this white paper, you'll learn how to:

  • Assign the six recurring roles behind successful deployments, from executive sponsor to QA and operations analyst
  • Decide whether to build, buy, or partner; comparing hands-on vendor partnership, self-serve builds, and in-house orchestration
  • Prepare the four categories of integrations every agent depends on: communication channels, customer data, tools and workflows, and knowledge content
  • Design escalation paths and fallback behavior for when dependent systems are slow or unavailable
  • Roll out in phases — piloting on a small, low-risk slice of traffic before widening deployment against pre-set gates
  • Run a standing improvement rhythm of reviewing outcomes, shipping A/B-tested changes, and expanding your test suite
  • Scale across six vectors — traffic, segment, capability, use case, channel, and region — without rebuilding your agent from scratch
  • See how real deployments (Rohlik, a European postal service, 3Shape, CARS24) applied this model to cut costs, reduce call abandonment, and automate a growing share of business volume
